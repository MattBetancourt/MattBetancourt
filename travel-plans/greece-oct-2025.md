## 🏨 Romantic Hotel Options

### Splurge — The Iconic Choice

**Hotel Grande Bretagne** (Syntagma Square)
- Historic landmark, 150 years of hospitality
- Rooftop restaurant with direct Acropolis views
- Rooftop pool, spa, Michelin-level dining
- **~€350–500/night** in October

### Boutique Luxury

**The Dolli at Acropolis** ⭐ 9.4
- Ultra-chic, modern, at the foot of the Acropolis
- Rooftop with stunning Parthenon views
- **~€650–750/night**

**AthensWas Design Hotel**
- Sleek design, Acropolis views from rooms
- Walking distance to Plaka
- **~$270/night**

### Mid-Range Romantic — Best Value

**Shila Athens** ⭐ 9.4
- Boutique, stylish, private garden access
- Very romantic, quiet, central
- **~$268/night**

**Mona Athens** (Monastiraki) ⭐ 9.2
- 20 rooms in a converted 1950s textile factory
- Speakeasy bar, rooftop, unique vibe
- **~$220/night**

**Perianth Hotel** (Agias Eirinis Square)
- Art Deco building, Bauhaus-inspired rooms
- Penthouse Suite has 360° views of Athens
- **~$200–300/night**

### Coastal Romantic

**The Margi** (Athens Riviera / Vouliagmeni)
- On the coast, 20 min from center
- Beach club vibe, pool, spa, sea views
- **~$250–350/night**

## 🎯 Top Pick

**Shila Athens** or **Mona Athens** — ~$220–270/night. Boutique, romantic, central (Plaka/Monastiraki). Walking distance to Acropolis, rooftop dinners, charming streets. ~$880–1,080 total for 4 nights.

## 🏛️ What to Do in Athens (4 Days)

- **Acropolis & Parthenon** — go early morning to beat crowds
- **Plaka** — charming old neighborhood, great for wandering & dinner
- **Monastiraki Flea Market** (Sundays)
- **Ancient Agora** — less crowded than Acropolis, equally fascinating
- **National Archaeological Museum** — world-class collection
- **Lycabettus Hill** — sunset views over the whole city
- **Rooftop dinner** — book a table with Acropolis view (GB Roof Garden, A for Athens, etc.)
- **Day trip option**: Cape Sounion & Temple of Poseidon (1 hr drive, sunset)

---

## 🌤️ October Weather in Athens

- **Daytime**: 20–24°C (68–75°F) — perfect for sightseeing
- **Evenings**: 14–18°C (57–64°F) — bring a light jacket
- **Rain**: 5–7 days/month, scattered showers not all-day
- **Sea**: 22–23°C — swimmable if you're brave, but not the main draw

## 💡 Tips

- Book hotels in advance for the best boutique options
- Most museums/sites are open full hours in October
- No need to rent a car — Athens is walkable + metro
- Tipping: 5–10% at restaurants is standard
- Learn a few Greek words: *Yassas* (hello), *Efharisto* (thank you)
